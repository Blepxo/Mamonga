Mamonga

Momonga (モモンガ) is a secondary character of the Chiikawa manga and anime series. They are a flying squirrel who is often seen begging for attention and praise, stealing food, and causing trouble for other characters. Their name comes from the Japanese word "momonga", which aside from being the name of a species of flying squirrel, also means Evil.
